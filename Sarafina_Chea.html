About Me

Hi! My name is Sarafina Chea. I am 21 years old and currently studying at the University of Michigan in the AMDP program at the School of Information (UMSI).

After graduating, I would love to move to California and begin the next chapter of my career there.

Fashion & Sewing

I am a dressmaker and tailor with a love for creating clothing. Sewing is one of my biggest passions, and I also teach sewing classes to help others learn how to make and alter their own garments.

I am especially interested in fashion and beauty, and I love exploring clothing as a form of creativity and self-expression.

Things I Love

Penpalling

I am super into penpalling and love writing and exchanging letters with people around the world. I enjoy getting to know people from different places and learning about their everyday lives and cultures through letters.

Travel

Traveling is another huge interest of mine. I have visited 13 countries, including the United States, and I hope to continue adding more destinations to that list.

My Favorite Place

Of all the places I have traveled, Vietnam is my favorite. I love experiencing new cultures, food, fashion, and everyday life while traveling, and Vietnam has been especially memorable to me.
